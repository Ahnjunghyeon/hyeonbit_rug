# Current artwork and attribution

Eleven custom painted breathing animations are loaded through breathing-catalog.js. They are not original PixelFX sprites. All runtime atlases use 32 frames, 6 columns, 480×240 physical tiles, logically 320×160 (1.5× density). Motion interpolation and bounded contact stencils are prepared offline.

The current insect-v4.webp refines the lavender needle thrust and butterfly wake. Its contact stencils stay inside the needle lane. serpent-v3.webp and sound-v3.webp are the approved redesigns. Other effects retain their existing artwork, including stone.webp. The separate temporary Gyomei GIF has not been applied.

Original PixelFX reference: https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx at commit 044e12a45dc9cc312cec50ee606dfc98afe5adc2. See ../licenses/PixelFX-MIT.txt. The original rug/physics source attribution and MIT notice remain in ../LICENSE and source.

Unused legacy PNG sprites and unversioned artwork aliases were removed. Attribution remains even though those assets are no longer shipped.
